# Term Research Proposal Guidelines

## Generative Second Language Acquisition (SLA)

## 1. Overview

This assignment requires you to design a **small-scale original research proposal grounded in generative approaches to SLA**. Your proposal should identify a clear theoretical problem in generative SLA, formulate well-defined research questions, and outline a methodologically appropriate study informed by existing generative literature. The goal is to demonstrate your ability to connect **generative theory** with **empirical research design**.

---

## 2. Core Principles of Generative SLA

Your proposal must be explicitly grounded in generative linguistics. Appropriate topics include:

* Accessibility of **Universal Grammar (UG)** in adult L2 acquisition
* Acquisition of **syntactic and semantic features**, parameters, and functional categories
* **Interface issues** in L2 acquisition, such as syntax–semantics and syntax–pragmatics
* **Feature reassembly** and form–meaning mappings
* Representational vs. processing-based explanations of L2 behavior
* **L2 processing**, including how learners use grammatical knowledge during real-time comprehension or production
* **Cross-linguistic influence (CLI)** under UG constraints

Avoid purely pedagogical, sociocultural, or behavioral approaches unless they directly engage with generative theory.

---

## 3. Required Structure

### 3.1 Topic

* Provide a **1–2 sentence statement** clearly identifying:

  * the L2 phenomenon
  * the **generative construct(s)** involved, such as UG access, parameter setting, feature reassembly, interface vulnerability, or L2 processing.

* The topic must be theoretically motivated, not instructional in nature.

---

### 3.2 Introduction

**Theoretical Problem**

* Describe the generative SLA issue your study addresses, such as UG accessibility, feature deficits, parameter resetting, interface vulnerability, or real-time access to grammatical representations.
* Explain why this problem matters for generative theory.

**Study Purpose**

* State how your study addresses this problem.
* Specify:

  * the target feature(s) or structure(s)
  * the L1–L2 pairing
  * why this pairing is theoretically informative.

---

### 3.3 Literature Review

* Review **key generative theories** relevant to your topic, such as FRH, RDH, MSIH, Feature Reassembly, the Interface Hypothesis, or processing-based accounts of L2 behavior.
* Summarize **empirical findings** from generative SLA studies.
* Identify:

  * theoretical gaps, such as unresolved debates
  * methodological gaps, such as overreliance on offline tasks
  * cross-linguistic gaps
  * processing-related gaps, such as limited evidence on whether learners can access target-like representations in real time.

* Clearly state **how your study addresses these gaps**.

---

### 3.4 Research Question(s)

* Pose **one primary research question** that directly tests a generative hypothesis.
* Optional: include 1–2 secondary questions.
* Questions must target **linguistic representations**, **real-time access to those representations**, or the relationship between representation and processing.
* Avoid questions that only ask about surface accuracy, classroom preference, or general pedagogy.
* Explicitly connect the question(s) to the identified gap(s).

---

### 3.5 Methodology

Your methods must be **generatively motivated**.

**Method**

* Specify the overall design, such as experimental, corpus-based, or mixed-methods.
* Justify task choice with reference to generative SLA research.

**Participants**

* Define L1–L2 pairing, proficiency levels, and sample size.
* Justify all choices theoretically.

**Tasks & Data**

* Describe tasks, such as:

  * grammaticality judgment task (GJT)
  * acceptability judgment task (AJT)
  * truth-value judgment task (TVJT)
  * elicited production
  * interpretation task
  * self-paced reading
  * eye-tracking
  * ERP or other processing measures, where appropriate.

* Explain how each task tests the target generative construct.

**Analysis**

* Specify:

  * dependent and independent variables
  * coding procedures
  * statistical or qualitative analysis procedures.

* Explain how different outcomes would support competing generative accounts.

---

### 3.6 Expected Results and Discussion (Optional)

* Briefly outline expected patterns based on generative theory.
* Discuss how alternative outcomes would be interpreted theoretically.
* Note key limitations.

---

### 3.7 Conclusion (Optional)

* Restate the central theoretical question.
* Summarize the study’s contribution to generative SLA.
* Suggest directions for future research.

---

### 3.8 References

* Minimum **15 scholarly sources** in generative SLA.
* Use a consistent academic citation style.

---

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be assessed on:

1. Clarity of **generative theoretical alignment**
2. Depth and accuracy of **literature engagement**
3. Quality and relevance of the **research question(s)**
4. **Methodological appropriateness** for testing generative hypotheses
5. Overall coherence and scholarly writing quality

---

1. **Language**: The proposal may be written in English or Chinese.

4. **File Format**: Submit **PDF only**.
