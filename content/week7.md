### 🔍 Core ideas
- **Movement & placement**: Many word-order differences (adverb/negation position, subject–aux inversion, clause-final verbs) are **movement-driven**; learners’ L1 settings predict early **placement errors**.
- **Dependencies & constraints**: **Wh-movement** and **relative clauses** create **filler–gap** dependencies; **islands** constrain movement—use these to test whether L2 grammars encode **abstract constraints**.
- **Competing accounts**:
  - **Representational deficit**: Certain **functional features** (e.g., strong movement triggers) are **absent or unrecoverable** → persistent optionality even with time/practice.  
  - **Full representation + processing** (e.g., SSH): Target-like features **are present**, but **time pressure** or **memory load** yields **shallower** online structure-building, leading to slower RTs or more errors under speed.

### 🗂️ Mini-lecture outline (Session 1, 45 min)

#### 1) Word order & movement diagnostics (V-to-T, V2, head direction)
- **Key notes**:  
  - **V-to-T**: French allows finite verbs to appear before adverbs/negation (V raises), English typically doesn’t (finite main verbs stay below Adv/Neg; auxiliaries move).  
  - **V2**: Germanic V2 puts the finite verb in second position in **main** clauses (not the same in embedded clauses).  
  - **Head direction**: OV vs. VO / head-final vs. head-initial affects verb–object order and placement of particles/clauses.  
- **Class activity**: Label the **diagnostic region** in minimal pairs (e.g., *Jean (souvent) mange des pommes* vs. *John often eats apples* / *John eats often apples*).

#### 2) Long-distance dependencies (wh, relatives) & island constraints
- **Key notes**:  
  - **A′-movement** (wh/fronting) vs. **A-movement** (passive/raising).  
  - **Islands**: wh-island (embedded Q), complex NP (relative clauses / complements of nouns), subject islands.  
  - Use island violations to ask if L2 grammars respect **abstract constraints** (competence) even if online **reanalysis** is costly.  
- **Example**: Compare acceptability for wh-extraction from complements vs. from **island** positions.

#### 3) Competing accounts & predictions (deficit vs. processing)
- **Key notes**:  
  - **Deficit/failed features** → **persistent** misplacement or acceptance of ungrammatical extraction **even in untimed, highly explicit tasks**.  
  - **Full representation + processing** → **native-like judgments** in untimed tasks but **slower RTs** / more regressions in online tasks, especially in **complex** environments.  
- **Quick demo**: Untimed AJT vs. timed AJT on the same contrasts—predict which differences will surface only under time pressure.

> **Pacing**: Three focused segments (~12 min each) + ~9 min discussion on where your L1 would create the strongest predictions.

### 🧪 Methods micro-lab (Session 2, 45 min)
**Goal**: Design a compact test for one syntactic property that distinguishes **representation** from **processing** explanations.

Pick **ONE** property and complete the template:

- **Property A — V-to-T (adverb/negation placement)**  
  - *Groups*: L1 French vs. L1 Mandarin → L2 English (matched proficiency).  
  - *Task*: **AJT + SPR** on adverb placement (e.g., *often* position).  
  - *Predictions*:  
    - **Deficit** → persistent acceptance of *John eats often sushi* in AJT + limited improvement.  
    - **Processing** → native-like AJT but **longer RTs** at adverb region in SPR for the disallowed order.

- **Property B — V2 (main vs. embedded clauses)**  
  - *Groups*: L1 German vs. L1 Mandarin → L2 English.  
  - *Task*: **Elicited production** of declaratives/questions + **AJT** on embedded inversion.  
  - *Predictions*:  
    - **Deficit** → continued overuse of inversion in embedded clauses in both tasks.  
    - **Processing** → correct judgments offline, occasional production slips under speed/complexity.

- **Property C — Wh-movement & islands**  
  - *Groups*: L1 Mandarin (wh-in-situ) vs. L1 Spanish (wh-fronting) → L2 English.  
  - *Task*: **AJT** (grammatical vs. island violations) + **Eye-tracking** on wh-dependencies.  
  - *Predictions*:  
    - **Deficit** → acceptance of **island** extractions even in untimed AJT.  
    - **Processing** → reject island violations offline but show **prolonged regressions**/slowdowns at gap or disambiguation online.

**Deliverable**: 3-line prereg: hypothesis, **primary DV** (accuracy/acceptability, RTs, regressions), and **falsifier**.
